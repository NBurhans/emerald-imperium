## Parse gotchas — verify each, do not assume

1. **Values in `key:value` columns escape a literal colon as `\:`.** Split on the first UNESCAPED colon. Two species names need this — `Type: Null` and its lineage — and a naive `split(":")` silently returns the wrong form name for them.
2. **`ceiling_item_by_checkpoint` is the reproducible build column.** `ceiling_item_at_last_cp` is a convenience and is only correct at the last checkpoint a species is scored for; the ceiling item changes as Phase 2 unlocks better ones.
3. **`tier` alone is not safe to display for the 76 rows where `tier_fragile` is TRUE.** Those sit within 0.002 of a threshold and flip between two equally valid ways of averaging VORP. Show `tier_edge_distance` beside it.
4. **Stat array order is HP, Atk, Def, SPEED, SpA, SpD.** Speed is fourth, not last. Verified against 8 asymmetric vanilla lines, 8/8 match.
5. **levelUpMoves pairs are [move_id, level], move FIRST.**
6. **Evolution records are [method, target_species, parameter]** — target second, and the parameter is POLYMORPHIC. Method 4 = level, 7 = item id, 23 = MOVE id, 31 = partner species id, 43 = move id, 15 = beauty threshold. `evo_params` names the kind per record.
7. **move.acc has two sentinels:** 0 = no accuracy check (status), 999 = never misses.
8. **dex_number and internal_index diverge from index 906 onward** (629 of 1,534 rows); 218 dex numbers are shared by multiple form rows. Join on `internal_index`.
9. **Never join species on `species_name`.** A base form and its Mega share one name — `Venusaur` appears twice. Name-joining collapses forms and silently drops movepools; it dropped 510 of 1,534 here before it was caught. `internal_index` is exactly 1:1 against `imperium_layer.json` in both directions.
10. **`02_world.internal_id` is polymorphic by `entity_type`.** TM and HM rows store the **move** id, not an item id. 28 of those collide with real item ids — 211 is Fire Stone as an item and Steel Wing as a TM. Filter to non-TM rows before using it as an item key.
11. **`imperium_layer.json`'s `items` and `abilities` are ALPHABETICALLY SORTED NAME LISTS**, not id-indexed arrays. `items[211]` is not item 211.
12. **`imperium_layer.json` writes the Defense base stat as `df`**, the calc fork's internal compressed key. The public `Specie` interface wants `def`. Unremapped it is `undefined`, and calcs return NaN rather than raising.
13. **Imperium move data carries `flags.healing`** (36 moves) — use it rather than a name list. But it also marks DRAIN moves, which heal off damage dealt, not max HP. A wall that "recovers" only by draining is not a wall; the two are split in this analysis.
14. **02_world `cost` is only as good as the PokeMarts sheet.** itemData carries no prices, pockets or locations — it is id, name and description only.
15. **Ground items and hidden items appear in no supplied source.** Absence in 02_world is not evidence of absence in game.
16. **04_valuation `*_by_checkpoint` columns encode `checkpoint:value`**, comma-separated, e.g. `1:0.4213,2:0.4488`. `lineage_form_per_checkpoint` encodes `checkpoint:form_name`.

## Model assumptions

- **Composite value weights, v2** (on the workbook's Assumptions sheet and editable there): offense 0.26, defense 0.15, **sustain 0.16**, speed 0.12, utility 0.10, typing 0.11, niche fit 0.10. Defensive roles use sustain 0.24 and speed 0.04 instead, because a wall was paying full weight on a stat it does not want. The weight set is chosen per species by its MODAL role across the run, not per checkpoint, so the species mean is a single formula the workbook can reproduce.
- **SUSTAIN** is the turn dimension the five original axes could not see. Sweep depth across the roster on one lifebar, healing per turn from recovery / Regenerator / Leftovers, hazard and Toxic chip cutting turns-to-KO, screens cutting incoming damage 33%, and the lifebar resetting at each TRAINER boundary because the player heals between fights. The axis is the **kit delta**: the species with its kit minus the identical species with none. Raw sweep depth correlated 0.809 with BST and was rejected; the delta is 0.561 and is exactly zero for the 88 species with no kit.
- **The ceiling is the better of two spreads**: offensive (252 in the higher attacking stat plus speed) and defensive (252 HP plus the better defence, bulk nature, Leftovers). The defensive spread wins for 9,679 of 21,015 species-checkpoint pairs.
- **Replacement level** is the third-best obtainable species for that role at that checkpoint under zero investment (neutral nature, 0 EVs, 31 IVs, first ability, no item, level-up moves only). 394 (checkpoint, role) cells; 14 fell back to the worst qualifier because the role had fewer than three, and say so in `replacement_basis`.
- **Tier thresholds on mean ceiling VORP**, not curve-forced: S+ ≥ 0.20, S ≥ 0.14, A ≥ 0.09, B ≥ 0.04, C ≥ 0.00, D ≥ −0.06, F below. Result: S+ 2, S 41, A 103, B 217, C 219, D 317, F 412.
- **`cant_miss` rule, written down rather than vibed:** tier A or better AND (`one_time_only` OR `missable`). Both halves required — a missable dud is not a priority, and a great species you can always get later is not either. 38 species.
- **`investment_cost` has no EV-training or breeding component**, per the RUN_CONFIG decision to drop the eight unconfirmable fields. It is built from nature (+1.0), held item (+1.0), ability change (+1.0), evolution item (+1.0), gated TMs (+0.5 each, cap 2.0) and egg moves (+2.0, because RUN_CONFIG says breeding is not used in this run, so an egg-move ceiling is out of reach rather than free), floored at 0.5. **ROI figures are therefore not comparable to a version of this analysis with a full cost model.**
- **Ceilings are gated by Phase 2.** TMs available ramp 3 → 174 across the ladder and held items 1 → 14, so no ceiling rests on an item the player cannot yet hold.
- **Percentile normalisation is within the obtainable pool at each checkpoint**, not min-max across the dex, so legendaries do not compress everyone else.
- The checkpoint ladder is a **reconstruction**. RUN_CONFIG cites the Boss Battles `Main` tab as its source; that sheet is not among the files this analysis holds. 17 of 19 caps anchor to a fixed-level boss actually present in the data; cp02 and cp09 are linear interpolations. The ladder is strictly monotonic and runs 15 → 85, which is RUN_CONFIG's only available cross-check.
- Gen 6+ mechanics inferred from Fairy, the Steel/Ghost/Dark chart, and per-move category; generation pinned to 9. The damage formula generation is unconfirmed, so **every Phase 4 calc is Inferred**.
